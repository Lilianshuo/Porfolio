Alibaba／阿里巴巴实习 · 母婴购物Alibaba／阿里巴巴 internship · maternal & infant shopping

儿童年龄是不是有用的用户分层信号？Is a child’s age a useful segmentation signal?

用户与业务问题User and business problem
母婴购物者的需求会随孩子年龄变化，但能不能按孩子的年龄来分层推荐，值不值得为此投入？Maternal and infant shoppers’ needs change with a child’s age, but can recommendations be segmented by it, and is that worth building for?
我的判断My judgement
年龄只能当粗粒度的辅助信号：只有 3.2% 的记录带年龄标签，购买行为也无法可靠反推孩子的年龄。Age works only as a coarse auxiliary signal: just 3.2% of records carry an age label, and purchase behaviour does not reliably reveal a child’s age.
方案与取舍Options and trade-offs
按销量还是买家渗透率排序（批量订单扭曲了销量，所以选了渗透率）；分类模型对比基线；按年龄的 Top-K 对比全局热门。Rank by sales or by buyer penetration (bulk orders distorted sales, so penetration); classifiers against a baseline; age-based Top-K against global popularity.
优先级依据Priority basis
按预期效果与实施难度排序：先做短期、低投入的年龄筛选与按年龄分组的评价；需要先做用户研究的安全与适龄信息放到中期。Top-20 只高 5.3 个百分点且不稳定，所以任何线上测试都排在这些步骤之后。Ordered by expected effect and effort: short-term, low-effort age filters and age-grouped reviews first; safety and age-suitability information, which needs user research first, in the medium term. Top-20 is only 5.3 points ahead and not stable, so any online test comes after those steps.
范围与交付物Scope and deliverables
离线分析：数据清洗、年龄分类实验、Top-K 评估（单次划分，假设真实年龄已知），以及分析与可视化、模型对比、给团队的页面设计建议表。Offline analysis: data cleaning, an age-classification experiment and a Top-K evaluation (one split, true age assumed known), delivered as analysis and visualisations, a model comparison and a table of page-design suggestions for the team.
对决策的影响Effect on the decision
建议把年龄当作粗粒度的辅助信号，先做低投入的年龄筛选与按年龄分组的评价，线上测试放在其后；团队是否采纳尚未确认。Recommended using age as a coarse auxiliary signal, starting with low-effort age filters and age-grouped reviews, with any online test after them. Whether the team adopted this is not confirmed.
下一步验证Next validation
访谈家长，了解他们在不同阶段看重什么、信任哪些年龄与安全信息；在真实页面上做年龄筛选和分组评价的可用性测试；之后才考虑 A/B 测试。Interview parents about what they weigh at each stage and which age and safety information they trust; usability-test age filters and age-grouped reviews on real pages; only then consider an A/B test.
  • 研究结论：有Research finding: yes
  • 设计建议：有Design suggestion: yes
  • 团队采纳：未确认Team adoption: not confirmed
  • 上线效果：无Launch result: none

Calm by Default · 个人硕士项目Calm by Default · individual MSc project

在不让学生“失联”的前提下降低通知焦虑Reducing notification anxiety without cutting students off

用户与业务问题User and business problem
学生既要保持可联系，又被通知打断、查看后停不下来；产品怎样减轻压力而不让人“失联”？Students need to stay reachable but are pulled in by notifications and keep using the phone after checking; how can a product reduce that pressure without cutting people off?
我的判断My judgement
问题不在“屏蔽更多”，而在“保持可达，但不必频繁查看”。The problem is not “block more” but “stay reachable without having to keep checking”.
方案与取舍Options and trade-offs
对比了静音、灰度、定时批量、按边界管理等已有做法，各有代价；选择系统级的低操作模式，因为通知来自许多应用。I compared muting, greyscale, batching and boundary-based managers, each with a cost; I chose a system-level, low-effort mode because notifications come from many apps.
优先级依据Priority basis
以工作坊、问卷与可用性测试的证据排序：先保证重要消息可达，再减少查看的冲动。Ordered by evidence from workshops, a survey and a usability test: keep important messages reachable first, then reduce the urge to check.
范围与交付物Scope and deliverables
个人项目：研究、三项设计原则、可交互的 Figma 原型 Calm Profile V2，以及基于 6 人可用性测试的三项设计改进。Individual project: the research, three design principles, an interactive Figma prototype (Calm Profile V2) and three design refinements informed by a six-person usability test.
对决策的影响Effect on the decision
把目标从“屏蔽更多”改成“保持可达”，并据此确定系统级低操作模式，而不是又一个通知管理应用。Reframed the goal from “block more” to “stay reachable” and, on that basis, chose a system-level low-effort mode rather than another notification-management app.
下一步验证Next validation
在真实使用中做现场研究；临时例外（Temporary Bypass）只是未来可考虑的方向。Field research in real use; a temporary bypass is only a future consideration.
  • 研究结论：有Research finding: yes
  • 设计建议：有（原型）Design suggestion: yes (prototype)
  • 团队采纳：不适用Team adoption: n/a
  • 上线效果：无Launch result: none
