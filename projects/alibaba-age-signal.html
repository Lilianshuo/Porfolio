案例 03 · 阿里巴巴实习 · 行为数据研究Case study 03 · Alibaba internship · behavioural-data research

儿童年龄是不是有用的用户分层信号？Is a child’s age a useful segmentation signal?

内容平台正在分流淘宝的母婴品类。我检验了儿童年龄能否用来划分购物人群、在缺少年龄数据时购买行为能在多大程度上替代它，以及这个结论对推荐和页面设计意味着什么。Taobao’s maternal and infant category faces competition from content platforms. I tested whether a child’s age can segment shoppers, how far purchase behaviour can stand in for missing age data, and what the answer means for recommendations and page design.

研究问题儿童年龄能否作为母婴购物者的分层信号？它能为推荐和页面设计增加什么价值？Research questionCan a child’s age serve as a segmentation signal for maternal and infant shoppers, and what does it add to recommendations and page design?
我的角色数据清洗与探索；年龄分类实验；基于规则的 Top-K 推荐原型。My roleData cleaning and exploration; an age-classification experiment; a rule-based Top-K recommendation prototype.
数据与方法29,971 条交易记录（其中 955 条带有儿童年龄标签）。买家渗透率分析；对照 Dummy 基线的分类模型；离线 Top-K 评估。Data and methods29,971 transaction records (955 with a child-age label). Buyer-penetration analysis; classifiers against a dummy baseline; offline Top-K evaluation.
核心发现不同年龄段的品类偏好不同。年龄标签只覆盖 3.2% 的记录。仅凭购买行为很难预测年龄。在离线测试中，基于年龄的推荐列表在 Top-20 的命中率更高，但在 Top-5 和 Top-10 并非如此。Core findingsCategory preferences differ by age band. Age labels cover 3.2% of records. Purchase behaviour is a weak predictor of age. In an offline test, age-based lists had a higher hit rate at Top-20 but not at Top-5 or Top-10.
对决策的影响我建议把年龄作为粗粒度的辅助信号，与行为信号一起使用，并把发现整理成页面层面的建议，交给团队去验证。工作停留在离线阶段：没有上线，也没有 A/B 测试。Impact on decisionsI recommended using age as a coarse auxiliary signal alongside behaviour, and turned the findings into page-level suggestions for the team to test. The work stayed offline: no launch or A/B test.

01要决定的事The decision

在基于年龄的个性化上，值得投入多少产品精力。How much product effort to put behind age-based personalisation.

内容平台正在把家长从淘宝的母婴品类中分流出去。团队想知道，儿童年龄能否用来划分用户，进而改进推荐和信息体验。摆在面前的决定是：在基于年龄的个性化上，值得投入多少产品精力。Content platforms are drawing parents away from Taobao’s maternal and infant category. The team wanted to know whether a child’s age can be used to segment users, then to improve recommendations and the information experience. The decision at stake was how much product effort to put behind age-based personalisation.

范围Scope

这是一项行为数据研究。我没有访谈或测试家长，所以“家长关心什么”只是有待下一步验证的假设。This is a behavioural-data study. I did not interview or test with parents, so what parents care about is framed as hypotheses to test next.

02带标签的样本很小A small labelled sample

只有 3.2% 的记录带有儿童年龄标签，这限制了任何年龄特征能触达的范围。Only 3.2% of records carry a child-age label, which limits what any age feature can reach.

清洗后的数据集通过用户 ID 把儿童记录与交易关联起来。其中只有一小部分能匹配到可用的儿童年龄。The cleaned dataset linked child records to transactions by user ID. Only a small share could be matched to a usable child age.

带有可用儿童年龄标签的记录占比Share of records with a usable child-age label

带标签的样本集中在 1–6 岁。0–1 岁和 12–18 岁这两个年龄段太小，无法支撑明确的结论，所以建模时我合并了年龄段。The labelled sample is concentrated in ages 1–6. The 0–1 and 12–18 bands are too small to support firm conclusions, so I merged bands for modelling.

03用买家渗透率，而不是购买量Buyer penetration, not volume

大宗订单会扭曲购买量，所以我改用买家渗透率来比较。Bulk orders distort purchase volume, so I compared buyer penetration instead.

我最初的计划是比较各年龄组的购买量。探索数据后发现这样会产生误导：多数交易的数量是 1，但有 65 条记录的数量达到 100 或以上，2022 年 11 月的购买高峰更可能是由少数大额订单推动的，而不是来自广泛的需求。于是我改用买家渗透率：某品类的独立买家数除以该年龄组的独立用户数。它回答的是“这个阶段有多少人需要这个品类”，而不是“这个阶段买了多少”。My first plan was to compare purchase volume by age group. Exploring the data showed why that would mislead: most transactions have a quantity of 1, but 65 records have a quantity of 100 or more, and a purchase peak in November 2022 was more likely driven by a few large purchases than by broad demand. I switched to buyer penetration: unique buyers of a category divided by the age group’s unique users. It answers “how many people at this stage need this category”, not “how much did this stage buy”.

描述性分析显示了什么What the descriptive analysis showed

不同年龄组的品类偏好有差异：有些品类各组都常见，有些则只属于某个阶段。在这个子样本中，男孩和女孩之间的差异看起来较小，各年龄组的时间趋势也没有明显分化。数据中有 400 个至少有一位买家的年龄段×品类组合，其中至少一半只有一位用户购买。这是 955 条记录子样本中的描述性规律，并不能证明是年龄在驱动购买。Category preferences differed across age groups, with some categories common to all groups and others specific to a stage. Differences between boys and girls looked smaller in this subsample, and the time trends did not diverge clearly by age group. The data hold 400 age-band × category combinations with at least one buyer, and at least half of them were bought by a single user. This is a descriptive pattern in a 955-record subsample, not evidence that age drives purchasing.

04行为能揭示年龄吗？Can behaviour reveal age?

仅凭购买行为，并不能可靠地判断孩子的年龄。Purchase behaviour alone does not reliably reveal a child’s age.

年龄缺失时，仅凭购买行为能否判断孩子所处的阶段？我把年龄合并为三个区间（0–3、3–6、6–18），使用品类、性别、数量和购买时间作为特征。我先建立了一个“预测最常见类别”的 Dummy 基线，让每个准确率数字都有可以对照的参照，再在按用户分层的 80/20 划分上比较三个模型（测试集 n = 191 位用户）。When age is missing, could purchase behaviour alone indicate the child’s stage? I merged ages into three bands (0–3, 3–6, 6–18) and used category, gender, quantity and purchase timing. I built a “most frequent class” dummy baseline first, so that every accuracy figure had something to be compared against, then compared three models on a user-level stratified 80/20 split (test n = 191 users).

我的判断My read

基于这份样本和这些特征，交易行为并不能可靠地反映孩子的年龄，所以我没有把它作为年龄预测能力提出来。直接收集或确认年龄是更可靠的做法。With this sample and these features, transaction behaviour does not reliably reveal a child’s age, so I did not propose it as an age-prediction capability. Collecting or confirming age directly is the more dependable route.

05已知年龄有帮助吗？Does a known age help?

在一次划分中，已知年龄在 Top-20 有帮助，但在 Top-5 或 Top-10 没有。In one split, a known age helped at Top-20 but not at Top-5 or Top-10.

撇开分类器，我问了一个更简单的问题：如果孩子的年龄已知，它能否帮助决定展示哪些品类？对每个年龄段，我只用训练集用户按买家渗透率对品类排序，再用留出的测试用户来检验。“命中”指测试用户实际购买过的品类中，至少有一个出现在其所在年龄段的前 K 位里。我把它与用同一批训练用户生成的、同样长度的全局热门榜做了对比。Separately from the classifier, I asked a simpler question: if a child’s age is known, does it help choose which categories to show? For each age band I ranked categories by buyer penetration using only the training users, then checked the held-out test users. A “hit” means at least one category the test user actually bought appears in the top K for their own age band. I compared this with a global-popularity list of the same length, built from the same training users.

我从中得到的结论What I took from it

在这次划分中，基于年龄的推荐在 Top-20 的命中率高出 5.3 个百分点，而热门基线在 Top-5 和 Top-10 略好。这提示在覆盖面更广的推荐中可能有收益，但并不能证明优势是一致的。这只是一次离线命中率检查，只有一次划分、191 位用户，并假设每位用户的真实年龄段已知；它不衡量点击、转化或收入，所以我把建议定得比较克制。Age-based recommendations showed a 5.3 percentage-point higher hit rate at Top-20 in this split, while the popularity baseline performed slightly better at Top-5 and Top-10. This suggests a possible benefit at broader recommendation coverage, but does not establish a consistent advantage. It is an offline hit-rate check on a single split of 191 users, assuming each user’s true age band is known; it is not a measure of clicks, conversion or revenue, which is why I kept the recommendation modest.

06建议Recommendation

把年龄作为粗粒度的辅助信号，并提出页面改动供团队验证。Use age as a coarse auxiliary signal, with page changes for the team to test.

年龄更适合回答“这个阶段可能需要什么”，而不是“这个人现在想要什么”。所以我建议把它作为粗粒度的辅助信号。下表是我给团队的设计建议，供其测试。Age seems better at answering “what might this stage need” than “what does this person want now”. So I recommended it as a coarse auxiliary signal. The table below holds my design suggestions for the team to test.

07局限Limits

只做了一次划分的离线检查，没有对家长做研究。An offline check on one split, with no research with parents.

  • 955 条记录的子样本可能不能代表全部用户；0–1 岁和 12–18 岁两档样本非常小。The 955-record subsample may not represent all users; the 0–1 and 12–18 bands are very small.
  • 品类只有 ID 而没有名称，限制了对结果的解读。Categories are IDs without names, which limited interpretation.
  • 只有一次随机划分，测试用户 191 位；没有重复划分，也没有置信区间。One random split with 191 test users; no repeated splits or confidence intervals.
  • 没有定性研究。家长是否想要基于年龄的信息、会信任什么，目前并不清楚。No qualitative research. Whether parents want age-based information, and what they would trust, is unknown.

我提出的下一步Next steps I proposed

  1. 访谈家长，了解在孩子不同阶段，什么在驱动他们的选择，以及他们信任哪些年龄或安全信息。Interview parents about what drives choices in different child stages, and which age or safety information they trust.
  2. 在真实页面上对年龄筛选和按年龄分组的评价做可用性测试。Usability-test age filters and age-grouped reviews on real pages.
  3. 只有在完成上述步骤、并约定好结果指标之后，才做 A/B 测试；同时提升年龄数据的覆盖率，并检查隐私透明度。Run an A/B test only after those steps, with agreed outcome metrics; improve age-data coverage and check privacy transparency.
