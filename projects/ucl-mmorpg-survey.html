社会因素与游戏内购买Social factors and in‑game purchases

一项面向 MMORPG 玩家的在线问卷，关于购买游戏内物品背后的社会性原因，以及社交焦虑是否与之有关。统计检验结果均不显著。这项研究展示的是：一个关于玩家动机的问题是如何设计的，以及我如何谨慎地判断 19 位玩家的数据能说明什么、不能说明什么。An online survey of MMORPG players about the social reasons behind buying in-game items, and whether social anxiety relates to them. The statistical tests were not significant. What the study shows is how a player-motivation question was designed, and how carefully I read what 19 players’ data can and cannot support.

研究问题社会因素如何影响 MMORPG 玩家的游戏内购买意愿？它们与玩家的社交焦虑有什么关系？Research questionHow do social factors influence in-game purchase intentions among MMORPG players, and how do they relate to players’ social anxiety?
我的角色个人研究项目，有导师指导。我参与确定研究问题，并参与材料与方法的设计，数据的收集与分析由我独立完成。My roleIndividual, supervised project. I contributed to the research questions and to the design of the materials and methods, and I collected and analysed the data independently.
方法与样本在线问卷（Qualtrics，通过 Prolific 招募），包含利博维茨社交焦虑量表（LSAS）与开放题；主题分析。34 人启动问卷，纳入分析 19 人。不是访谈。Method and sampleOnline survey (Qualtrics, recruited via Prolific) with the Liebowitz Social Anxiety Scale (LSAS) and open questions; thematic analysis. 34 started, 19 analysed. Not interviews.
发现玩家自己的叙述中反复提到购买的社会性原因，从同伴压力到展示身份。LSAS 得分与物品类型、概率型物品的购买经历或花费，均无显著关系。What it foundPlayers’ own accounts repeatedly cited social reasons for buying, from peer pressure to showing identity. LSAS scores did not significantly relate to item type, probability-based purchase experience or spending.
无法说明的事任何因果关系，或关于实际行为的结论：数据是自我报告，来自较小的便利样本，没有购买记录。What it cannot sayAnything causal, or about behaviour: the data are self-reported, from a small convenience sample, with no purchase records.

01玩家为什么买Why players buy

游戏内购买在一定程度上是社会性的，所以我问玩家为什么买。In-game purchases are partly social, so I asked players why they buy.

像 MMORPG 这样的游戏会出售对其他玩家有意义的物品：你的外观、你拥有什么、你在团队里能做什么。项目要问的是：玩家给出了哪些购买的社会性原因，这些原因是否因社交焦虑而不同。项目把概率型物品（PBI，如开箱）单独研究，因为它们带来了风险。Games such as MMORPGs sell items that matter to other players: how you look, what you own, what you can do in a group. The project asked what social reasons players give for buying them, and whether those reasons differ with social anxiety. It studies probability-based items (PBIs, such as loot boxes) separately, because they add risk.

  1. RQ1 社会因素如何影响玩家感知到的物品价值与购买原因？RQ1 How do social factors shape perceived item value and the reasons players buy?
  2. RQ2 社会影响如何左右玩家对功能型与装饰型物品的偏好？RQ2 How does social influence shape preference between functional and ornamental items?
  3. RQ3 在社交焦虑程度不同的玩家中，社会因素如何影响购买概率型物品的动机？RQ3 How do social factors shape motivation to buy PBIs, among players with different social anxiety?

02开放题问卷An open-question survey

动机需要听玩家自己的话。Motivations needed the players’ own words.

问卷可以让英国各地的玩家在自己的时间里作答，开放题让他们在没有预设类别引导的情况下描述购买。LSAS 则提供了一个衡量社交焦虑程度的标准参照。A survey reached players across the UK on their own time, and open questions let them describe purchases without preset categories steering them. LSAS gave a standard measure of social anxiety severity to compare against.

  • 先问开放题。除了关于玩家在游戏中做某些事的频率的选择题之外，题目改编自前人研究（Cai 等，2022；Lee 等，2018），改为开放题，例如让玩家回顾一次购买的经过，以及是什么引发了那种感受。Open questions first. Apart from multiple-choice questions on how often players do things in game, questions were adapted from earlier work (Cai et al., 2022; Lee et al., 2018) into open questions, such as walking through a purchase and what led to the feeling.
  • 措辞避免引起羞耻感。PBI 相关题目避免使用“赌博”一类的字眼，让玩家在描述自己实际做过的事时不会觉得被评判。Wording to avoid shame. The PBI questions avoided “gamble” wording so players would not feel judged when describing what they actually did.
  • PBI 的两条路径。有 PBI 经历的玩家描述自己的经历；没有的玩家则被问到他们认为可能是什么在驱动这种购买。后者是预期而非经历，解读时也按这个标准区分。Two routes for PBIs. Players with PBI experience described it; players without were asked what they thought might motivate it. Those are expectations, not experiences, and are read that way.

LSASLSAS24 项（11 项社交互动，13 项公开表现）。每种情境分别评定恐惧（0–3）与回避（0–3），得分为总和。这里用来描述严重程度，而非诊断。24 items (11 social interaction, 13 public performance). Each situation rated for fear (0–3) and avoidance (0–3); the score is the sum. Used here to describe severity, not to diagnose.
试填与时长Piloting and length一次试填约用了 20 分钟；问卷设有 30 分钟的时限。A trial run took about 20 minutes; the survey had a 30-minute limit.
伦理Ethics先阅读信息表并同意；经 UCLIC 研究伦理委员会批准。Information sheet and consent first; approved by the UCLIC research ethics committee.
分析Analysis在 NVivo 14 中做归纳式主题分析（Braun & Clarke, 2006）：标出关键词，把编码聚合成主题和子主题。定量部分：对 LSAS 与购买相关变量做双样本 t 检验和 Spearman 相关。Inductive thematic analysis (Braun & Clarke, 2006) in NVivo 14: keywords highlighted, codes clustered into themes and subthemes. Quantitative: two-sample t-tests and Spearman correlations of LSAS against purchase variables.

04发现Findings

玩家的叙述中反复出现社会性原因，但分数并没有把焦虑与购买联系起来。Social reasons recurred in players’ accounts, but the scores did not link anxiety to buying.

RQ3 概率型物品RQ3 Probability-based items朋友抽到好东西、直播主、口碑传播、想通过独一无二的物品显得与众不同。Friends’ good draws, live streamers, word of mouth, wanting to stand out through unique items.稀有性与有吸引力的奖励；意识到这是一种令人上瘾、类似赌博的机制；惊喜等情绪上的强化。Rarity and attractive rewards; awareness of an addictive, gambling-like mechanism; emotional reinforcement such as surprise.

这些主题来自 19 位参与者，描述的是他们回答中出现的内容。它们不能说明这些原因在玩家中有多普遍。RQ3 包含了没有 PBI 经历的玩家对动机的猜测。Themes come from 19 participants and describe what appeared in their responses. They do not show how common these reasons are among players. RQ3 includes players without PBI experience guessing at motives.

LSAS 得分最低的七位参与者，全都表示没有 PBI 经历。论文把这点记录为一个有意思的规律；但上面的 t 检验并不显著，且只有 19 位参与者，所以不能把它解读为社交焦虑与购买 PBI 之间存在关系。回答“不确定”的玩家被算作没有 PBI 经历。The seven participants with the lowest LSAS scores all reported no PBI experience. The paper notes this as an interesting pattern; the t-test above was not significant, and with 19 participants it cannot be read as a relationship between social anxiety and PBI buying. Players who answered “not sure” were counted as having no PBI experience.

05我能主张什么，数据又无法支持什么What I would claim, and what the data cannot support

数据能够支持The data can support

  • 这个样本中的玩家经常给出购买物品的社会性原因，包括没有 PBI 经历的玩家。Players in this sample frequently gave social reasons for buying items, including players without PBI experience.
  • 这些原因在功能型与装饰型物品之间，类型有所不同。Those reasons differ in kind between functional and ornamental items.
  • 开放的提问形式让玩家用自己的话描述原因，包括稀有性和错失恐惧。The open format let players describe their reasons in their own terms, including rarity and fear of missing out.

数据无法支持The data cannot support

  • 社交焦虑能预测花费或 PBI 购买。That social anxiety predicts spending or PBI purchasing.
  • 因果结论，或关于实际购买行为的结论：所有数据都是自我报告。Causal claims, or claims about actual purchase behaviour: all data are self-report.
  • 普遍程度，或对一般玩家的结论。Prevalence, or conclusions for players in general.
深入查看Deep dive论文中说明的局限Limits stated in the paper
  • 只有自我报告，没有行为层面的购买数据。Self-report only; no behavioural purchase data.
  • 玩家所说的“错失恐惧”含义不明确。What players meant by “fear of missing out” is ambiguous.
  • 样本很小，只有 19 人，通过机会抽样招募。Small sample of 19, recruited by opportunity sampling.
  • 研究者对社会因素的侧重，可能影响了主题的生成方式；论文对此做了反思。Researcher emphasis on social factors may have shaped how themes were generated; the paper reflects on this.
  • 没有把游戏内花费与游戏外的真实金钱做比较。No comparison of spending with real money outside games.

论文还提出，游戏可以帮助玩家从购买记录中认识自己的偏好，并讨论了对 PBI 的监管。这些是作者的讨论观点，不是经过检验的结果。The paper also suggests that games could help players see their own preferences from purchase history, and discusses regulation of PBIs. These are the author’s discussion points, not tested results.
